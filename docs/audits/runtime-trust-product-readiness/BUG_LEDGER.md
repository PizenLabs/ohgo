## Documented limitations (not defects disguised as fixes)

- **Multiple sibling Go modules** are inventoried and parsed, but imports are
  not resolved across module boundaries. The ambiguity is now explicit.
- **Non-Go semantic resolution** remains syntax-level. Tree-sitter adapters do
  not perform type resolution, overload resolution, or dispatch.
- **GRY edge freshness** cannot be proven from trace payloads alone. The
  adapter verifies endpoints against current source; it does not claim every
  edge is current.
- **Claim audit vocabulary** is finite. A plausible unsupported claim outside
  the audited shapes can still pass; the tested classes are covered.
- **Runtime-state sanitization** applies to the final answer, not to live
  streaming deltas. The persisted transcript and completion input are
  sanitized.
